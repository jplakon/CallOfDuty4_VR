# Release permission record

Keep the actual Infinity Ward correspondence in a private, backed-up location.
Do not commit private email addresses, signatures, internal contact details, or
the correspondence itself to the public repository unless the sender
explicitly authorized publication.

Record privately:

- Date permission was granted
- Name and role of the person granting it
- The exact project/version covered
- Whether charging through Patreon is covered
- Whether distributing a modified executable is covered
- Territory and duration
- Whether screenshots, video, logos, and game title use are covered
- The exact public wording you are allowed to use
- Any required disclaimer, attribution, takedown, or approval process

The public project should continue to say it is an independently developed mod
and not an official Call of Duty product unless the permission expressly
authorizes a different statement.
